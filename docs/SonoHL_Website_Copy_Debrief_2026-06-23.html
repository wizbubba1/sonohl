Website copy remediation debrief

SonoHL Website Alignment

Date: June 23, 2026  |  Commit: 486f22c  |  Source: A0-audited whitepaper v0.1 DRAFT

Outcome. The entire website narrative was changed from promotional medical-product copy to controlled investigational-development language. Unsupported diagnostic, performance, configuration, trial-status, remote-readiness, and value claims were removed or recast as hypotheses. The visual structure and existing single-file implementation were preserved.

What Changed

AreaRemediationReason
Product positioningReframed SonoHL as an “investigational acoustic-sensing platform in development.” Added that the product is not available for commercial sale.The intended use, configuration, clinical output, and regulatory pathway are not yet approved.
Diagnostic and screening claimsRemoved “AI-powered screening,” diagnostic screening, defect detection, and diagnostic/telemonitoring product positioning.These claims require an approved intended use, clinical evidence, and applicable regulatory authorization.
Performance claimsRemoved claims of automatic clearest-signal selection, real-time analysis, noise reduction, clearer recordings, and abnormal-sound detection.No controlled specifications, acceptance criteria, verification reports, or performance results were supplied.
Device configurationRemoved factual 16-channel, 10-front/6-back, 8+, and 8-sensor statements. Diagrams are now labeled illustrative.The source materials contained conflicting configurations; the current controlled device version is unresolved.
Epidemiology and trial statusRemoved the 2.5 million, 40%, and “CT1 currently enrolling” statements.The audit did not verify exact primary sources or a current controlled trial record.
Remote useRecast telemonitoring and home/remote use as future evaluation concepts with explicit dependencies.Remote readiness requires validated setup, transmission, privacy, cybersecurity, review, and escalation workflows.
Clinical workflowChanged the workflow to prepare/fit, acquire/transfer, and qualified human review. Added controls still required at each stage.The original simplified workflow implied operational readiness and established clinical use.
Evidence pageReplaced unsupported development-status claims with a technical, clinical, human-factors, and workflow evidence framework.Prototype, validation, pilot, and regulatory statuses require verified records and approvals.
Team and governanceMarked titles, affiliations, roles, and governance wording as pending verification.The audit did not independently confirm current titles, institutional wording, or publication permission.
Partnership outreachChanged promotional investment/diagnostic language to controlled product and evidence-development collaboration.External outreach must disclose investigational status and distinguish objectives from demonstrated results.

Why This Is a Material Improvement

  • Reduced regulatory exposure: the public narrative no longer presents investigational objectives as established safety, effectiveness, diagnostic, or commercial facts.
  • Improved internal consistency: conflicting sensor counts and development stages were replaced with controlled-input requirements.
  • Clearer product boundaries: the site now consistently states that human review is required and that SonoHL does not independently diagnose disease or replace medical judgment.
  • Better evidence discipline: technical validation, clinical validity, human factors, privacy, cybersecurity, and workflow evidence are separated rather than blended into promotional promises.
  • More defensible partnership language: collaborators are invited to support evidence generation without implying market readiness or demonstrated clinical benefit.

Scope note: This remediation changed website copy and accessibility labels only. It did not refactor the HTML, CSS, JavaScript, embedded images, hosting, analytics, or underlying product implementation.
